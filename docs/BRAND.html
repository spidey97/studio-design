01 · Foundation

Why DesignStudio exists

Makers jump between a design tool, a motion tool, a media editor and a 3D app, exporting and re-importing at every step. DesignStudio keeps all four in one studio, linked, and fully offline, so an idea can move from screen to motion to space without leaving the room.

Purpose

Give every maker one connected place to design, move and build, without uploads or hand-offs.

Promise

Everything connects. A change in Design shows up in Media and 3D. Nothing gets lost between tools.

Audience

UI/UX designers, motion designers and creative developers who prototype across 2D, motion and 3D, often on a laptop and often offline.

Positioning

For designers and motion artists who juggle separate tools, DesignStudio is the offline studio where design, media and 3D stay linked, so work moves from screen to motion to space with no exports, uploads or subscriptions.

Brand pillars

Connected

Links between Design, Media and 3D. The yellow node is this idea made visible.

Crafted

Precise tools, honest feedback, real keyframes and real exports.

Private by default

Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose.

Calm

A quiet, dark workspace where your work is the brightest thing on screen.

What we learned
  • Every competitor owns one discipline. Work crosses tools through exports, plugins and re-imports.
  • Most run in the cloud. Few let a maker work fully offline without uploading files.
  • Visually the category is crowded with multicolour marks and abstract shapes. Few brands carry a story about connection.
  • Opportunity: own 'connected': one studio where design, motion, media and 3D stay linked, local by default.

03 · Voice & tone

How we write

Lead with the outcome

Say what the person gets first, then how.

Plain words, short sentences

One idea per sentence. Use "you", and avoid filler.

Name things exactly

Use the button's real label, the real format and the real number.

Errors help, not blame

What happened, why, and the one thing to try next.

Naming

Write the product name as DesignStudio: one word, capital D and S. The lowercase "designstudio" appears only in the logo. The sections are Design, Media and 3D. Write "Spideytech" on its own, as the maker: "DesignStudio by Spideytech".

04 · Logo

The wordmark

"designstudio" in lowercase heavy geometric letters, outlined from Sora Bold and modified. design carries the blue-to-violet gradient and studio is pearl. Both i-dots are holy-yellow link nodes: two dots that join the two words, the way the app joins Design, Media and 3D.

On dark (default): pearl studio, yellow nodes

On light: ink studio, deep-gold nodes

White: on brand colour, photos and video

Ink: print and one-colour use

Short form & app icon

The wordmark's own "d" with a holy-yellow node. Use it only where the wordmark can't fit: the favicon, app icons, avatars and the narrow top bar.

Clear space, size and misuse

Leave clear space equal to the height of the "d" on every side. The minimum height is 12 px on screen or 4 mm in print; below that, use the short form.

DesignStudioDESIGNSTUDIO

Don't slant, recolour, retype or capitalise it, don't split the words, and don't move the dots or put the wordmark on busy images without a dark scrim.

07 · Graphic language

The link-node network

The i-dot becomes a system: yellow nodes joined by thin pearl lines, like a constellation of connected work. Use it as texture behind content, always faded out where the words sit.

From left: node network · dot grid (24 px) · network on light · gradient glow (blue and violet, heavily blurred, at most two per layout).

Rules

Keep big nodes holy yellow and small nodes pearl at 50%; lines are pearl at 16%. Run at most one network per layout, and mask it away from text. Gradient glows sit behind, never in front of, content. Rounded corners follow the token scale: 6, 10, 14 and 22 px.

09 · Motion

How things move

Motion explains change: where something came from, what it's connected to and that an action worked. The signature move is the node pop, a small overshoot as a dot appears, used for links, saves and the splash.

11 · Files

Assets & tokens

  • Logo: brand/wordmark/ holds the wordmark on dark, on light, white and ink, the symbol, the small symbol and the app icon (SVG); brand/icon-512 / 192 / 32.png are the app icons.
  • Tokens: brand/tokens/tokens.css (CSS variables --ds-*), tokens.json and contrast.json.
  • Fonts: brand/fonts/ has Sora 400–800 and Inter 400–700 (woff2, SIL OFL).
  • Graphics: brand/graphics/ has the node networks and dot grids.
  • Applications: brand/applications/png/ holds the finished images and src/ the editable HTML sources.
  • Rebuild everything: run python3 brand/build_tokens.py, build_graphics.py, build_applications.py, build_book.py, then node tools/render-brand.mjs --pdf.
